HOW RESQMEAL WORKS

RECOVERY PROCESS

A structured flow that moves suitable surplus from declaration to verified recovery.

01

Declare Surplus

Approved donors record available surplus, quantity and veg or non-veg classification.

02

Find a Suitable NGO

Relevant NGO availability and recovery requirements are considered for matching.

03

Verify the Batch

The NGO in-charge physically checks the batch before recovery is enabled.

04

Complete the Recovery

Approved pickup is recorded and the recovered quantity is updated in the system.

CONNECTED PARTICIPANTS

RESQMEAL NETWORK

A coordinated network connecting approved donors, verified NGOs and administrative oversight.

DONOR SIDE

Approved Donors

Hotels, restaurants and event or party organizers can declare suitable surplus through the platform after approval.

RECEIVER SIDE

Verified NGOs

Approved NGOs can identify suitable recovery opportunities, verify batches and collect the required quantity.

CONTROL

Administrative Oversight

The administrator manages approvals, monitoring, verification records and system-level visibility.

SMART DECISION SUPPORT

INTELLIGENT RECOVERY LAYER

AI-assisted matching helps identify relevant recovery possibilities using practical recovery factors.

AI

Recovery Matching Support

The intelligent layer supports the recovery process by considering the available surplus, NGO requirements, location and recovery constraints instead of treating every donor-NGO combination as identical.

01

Quantity

Considers the quantity available and required.

02

Food Compatibility

Considers the declared food classification.

03

Distance

Considers practical donor-NGO proximity.

04

Recovery Window

Considers whether recovery can happen in time.

05

NGO Availability

Considers relevant NGO availability for pickup.

06

Remaining Quantity

Keeps unrecovered balance available for additional suitable matching.
