How Your NGO Uses ResQMeal

Your NGO can state its recovery requirements, review recommendations from approved donors, physically inspect assigned food, confirm collection and record distribution.

01 · Set Requirements

Specify food preferences, quantity, service area and required time.

02 · Review Matches

Assess recommended recovery opportunities and accept a suitable quantity.

03 · Complete Recovery

Complete the mandatory physical checks before pickup and distribution.
